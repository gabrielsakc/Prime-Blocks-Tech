Conclusion

They don't compete in the same category.

Edgevanta is good pricing software for estimators. RBCopilot is the DOT contractor's operating platform: from reading the bid package to collecting the last pay item, with the asphalt plant connected. Comparing the two is comparing a calculator with the system that runs the job.

Core message

“Edgevanta helps you win the bid. RBCopilot helps you make money on it — in the field, in claims and on every pay estimate.”

Leave them no opening

Today Edgevanta leads on multi-state data coverage, published customer stories and content. Publishing logos, case studies with numbers and a security page removes their only arguments.
